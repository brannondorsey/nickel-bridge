Three ways to move the trumps to the left

Players have asked, more than once, for their trump cards to sit at the left end of the fan once a trump contract is settled — in their own hand and in dummy — instead of staying in fixed ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ order. This board proposes three motions for that re-sort. Pick one and it ships behind a settings row, Trump placement · SUIT ORDER / LEFT SIDE.

Set the contract below and watch all three at once, or replay a single panel. The trump block is outlined here so you can follow it — that outline is a prototyping aid, not a proposed treatment.

What the re-sort actually is

The trump suit's block moves to the front; the other three keep their relative ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ order behind it, and ranks within every suit are untouched. Three consequences worth seeing on the board above, because they decide how much motion is actually worth building:

CaseWhat moves
Trump = ♠Nothing. Spades already lead the fan, so the animation must recognise a no-op and stay still rather than play an empty beat.
No-trumpNothing, ever. There is no trump suit to promote, so the setting has no effect on a NT contract and the fan keeps suit order.
Trump = ♥Only spades and hearts trade places — diamonds and clubs sit at exactly the same x before and after. A motion that moves every card is lying about how much changed.
Trump = ♣The whole fan shifts: clubs travel the full width, and all nine other cards slide right. This is the worst case, and the one to judge each motion by.

Where it happens, and to which hands

Only your own fan is on screen across the auction → play boundary, so it is the only hand that can be seen re-sorting. Everything else arrives already ordered:

HandTreatment
Your fanOn screen when the contract settles. This is what the three motions above are for — it plays once, on the beat between the final pass and the opening lead.
Dummy, as a fanTabled after the opening lead, so it is drawn trump-left from its first frame. No motion — animating a hand that was not previously on screen is movement with nothing to explain.
Dummy, as the side railThe E/W rail is four stacked suit rows, so "left" becomes "top": the trump row moves to the first line. It also arrives already ordered.
Analyze & the tourBoth replay finished boards through the same components, so they inherit whichever order the setting names — the replay's own scrubbing never re-triggers the motion.

Under prefers-reduced-motion, every proposal below collapses to the same thing: the fan is simply drawn in the new order on the frame the contract settles. There is no reading pause to preserve here — unlike the claim announcement or a held trick, nothing is being said, so nothing needs a beat.

The settings row, either way

One more row on the settings gate, in the shape every other row already takes — tracked-caps label, an italic aside saying what it does, a two-segment PrefSwitch. An account column (users.trump_placement) written through the existing POST /api/me/prefs, defaulting to SUIT ORDER — the behaviour the app has today, so the row reads as a way out rather than a change.
